Consulting

Strategic and research consultancy in AI, ethics, and social good

I work with institutions, non-profits, and research organisations on the design, funding, and governance of technology projects. My practice spans research design, grant strategy, workshop development, and policy scoping — with particular depth in AI ethics, government regulation, and emergent technology for social good.

Much of this work was conducted through Justice Matrix (2020–2024), a consultancy I founded to support non-profit and public-sector initiatives developing sustainable, socially responsible technology projects and policies.

I work across budgets from £25k to £3 million and am comfortable navigating complex multi-partner structures, institutional funders, philanthropic foundations, and community organisations simultaneously.

What I offer

Research design and grant strategy

Pre-award bid development, grant writing, partner scoping, and budget architecture. Post-award project management, compliance, reporting, and evaluation. Experienced with AHRC, UKRI, ESRC, Arts Council England, and philanthropic funders.

AI ethics and policy

Scoping, advisory, and workshop design for organisations working on responsible AI governance, regulation, and public accountability. Particular expertise in racial justice, surveillance, and the politics of datasets.

Participatory workshop design

Co-design of workshops for public engagement, community research, and institutional training. Methodologies include collaborative diagramming, sensing walks, and participatory dataset design.

Strategic technology consultancy

ESG and EDI strategy, change management, executive coaching, and values-based philanthropy initiatives for commercial and public-sector clients.
