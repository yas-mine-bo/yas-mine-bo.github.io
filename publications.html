Reciprocity Deficits: Observing AI in the Street with Everyday Publics
arXiv preprint · With Alex S. Taylor, Noortje Marres, Mercedes Bunz, Thao Phan, Maya Indira Ganesh, Dominique Barron, Rachel Coldicutt, Iain Emsley, Beatrice Gobbo, Louise Hickman, Manu Luksch, Bettina Nissen, Mukul Patel, Luis Soares

Explores the challenges and possibilities of everyday public engagement with AI in the situated environment of city streets. Drawing on perspectives from social and cultural studies of AI, Design Research, and STS, the paper examines the affordances of the street as a site for material participation in AI through design-based interventions: the creation of everyday AI observatories.
