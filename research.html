Research

Active and completed research projects

Generative AI in Cultural Archiving
PhD — University of the Arts London (CCI) · Ongoing

Full title: Generative AI in Cultural Archiving: Developing Technical Pipelines and Ethnographic Processes for Image Generation in the Context of Intangible Cultural Heritage. The research develops participatory methodologies for working with communities to co-create AI training datasets rooted in decolonial principles and indigenous data sovereignty frameworks. Case studies in Algeria and Aotearoa New Zealand. Technical stack: PyTorch, HuggingFace Diffusers, LoRA fine-tuning, Stable Diffusion XL, ComfyUI.

2022–
AI in the Street — Everyday Observatories
AHRC BRAID Programme / University of Warwick / UAL · Role: Researcher and Creative Technologist

Led the London Observatory across three city locations — Science Gallery (London Bridge), Martello Street Studios (London Fields), and Hermitage Community Moorings (Wapping). Developed and facilitated diagramming workshops, sensing walks, and collaborative drawing sessions to understand everyday public encounters with AI infrastructure. Co-authored the project's policy-facing report and the arXiv preprint Reciprocity Deficits. Findings presented at Newspeak House, London (January 2026). Partners: Greater Cambridgeshire Partnership, Nokia Bell Labs, LOTI, NESTA, Transport for West Midlands.

2023–26
AI and Racial Justice — JUST AI Fellowship
Ada Lovelace Institute · Visiting Fellow · AHRC funded

Led a research project examining AI and Racial Justice. Built a collaborative platform — the AI Justice Matrix — interrogating epistemological frameworks on which tech ethics is constructed. Hosted a community of practitioners working to influence policy and emergent technology development. Presented at international conferences including CPDP 2021 and 2022, and Transmediale 2022.

2020–23
Ways of Machine Seeing
Alan Turing Institute / RAi UK Skills Programme / LSBU / UCL Institute of Education / The Photographers' Gallery · Via Justice Matrix

Collaborative research project on the mechanics and politics of image datasets and computer vision literacy. Designed workshops on how image datasets are constructed, labelled, and used in AI systems, for education curriculum and public engagement. Collaborators: Geoff Cox (LSBU), Andrew Dewdney, Nicolas Malevé (Aarhus), Annie Davey (UCL), The Photographers' Gallery.

2022–23

University of the Arts London · Co-author

Annual data analysis and appraisal of ethnic disparities and anti-racism initiatives across universities in England. Co-authored with Prof. David Mba and Chris Lloyd-Bardsley. Covered by Times Higher Education. Editions: 2022, 2023, 2024.
