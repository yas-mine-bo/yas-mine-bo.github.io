Selected work

Projects across art, research, and public engagement

AI in the Street
AHRC BRAID / University of Warwick / UAL

Five-city participatory observatory project examining how everyday publics encounter AI in city streets. London Observatory co-lead. Developed diagramming and sensing walk methodologies across London, Coventry, Edinburgh, Cambridge and Logan, Australia.

2023–26
AI Justice Matrix
Ada Lovelace Institute — JUST AI Fellowship

Collaborative platform interrogating the epistemological frameworks on which tech ethics is constructed. Invited practitioners to map knowledge formation around AI practice. Presented at Transmediale 2022 and CPDP 2022.

2021–22
Ways of Machine Seeing
Alan Turing Institute / LSBU / The Photographers' Gallery

Public engagement project on the mechanics and politics of image datasets and computer vision. Workshop design for education curriculum and policy, with LSBU, UCL Institute of Education, and The Photographers' Gallery.

2022–23
Listening Structures
TiTiPi / Infrastructural Interactions

A reflexive methodology for interrogating data infrastructures, developed as a workshop practice and published as a chapter contribution. Examines what is included and excluded in the act of transcription and testimony.

2022
Entanglements
Arts Council England funded / UAL

Workshop series and lecture programme offering a collective analysis of public-sector technology contracts. Output: a collage of writing, scribbles, images, memes and links that express the injustice and absurdity of entanglement between state powers and private tech companies.

2021–22
Data Processing
Better Images of AI / LOTI

Image co-created at a Better Images of AI workshop with the London Office of Technology and Innovation. Available under CC-BY 4.0 in the Better Images of AI free library.

2023
Safety Meme

2021
Sentient Blobs
Interactive light sculptures — Secret Garden Party 2022

Interactive light sculptures exhibited at Secret Garden Party.

2022
radical⇌matter
University of Applied Arts Vienna / RCA London

Group exhibition and symposium, University of Applied Arts Vienna (AIL). Presented impulse lecture 'An Algerian Technoritual'. Part of PEEK-FWF funded artistic research project hosted by RCA London.

Generative AI + Intangible Cultural Heritage
PhD research — University of the Arts London

Doctoral research developing technical pipelines and ethnographic processes for image generation in the context of intangible cultural heritage. Case studies in Algeria and Aotearoa New Zealand. Decolonial methodology, participatory AI, indigenous data sovereignty.
